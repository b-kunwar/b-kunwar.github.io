I am a graduate student in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at Auburn University and a research assistant in the [Vehicle Systems, Dynamics, and Design Laboratory](https://www.vsddl.com/). I joined Auburn in Fall 2022.

I am fascinated by the possibility of aircraft learning from flight and improving their performance on their own. My PhD research lays the groundwork by exploring methods and tools that use flight data, with a human in the loop, to understand aircraft behavior and refine models and control systems.

After graduation, I hope to extend this work to aircraft that learn and improve from one flight to the next, while communicating their performance limitations to both expert and non-expert users—all autonomously or with minimal human supervision.

From 2019 to 2022, I worked as a drone design engineer at [Prokura Innovations](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/venturefund/dronescohort/prokurainnovations). With a small team of drone enthusiasts in Nepal, I designed, built, and tested a lift-plus-cruise vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) fixed-wing UAV to deliver emergency medicines to rural communities. This hands-on experience motivated me to pursue graduate study and explore how flight data can inform aircraft development.

I was born and raised in a village in Baglung, Nepal. My education took me to schools progressively farther from home, a journey that reminds me of a line from The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: “You were born to the rolling hills and little rivers of the Shire, but home is now behind you. The world is ahead.”

Outside of research, I love running, cycling, hiking, playing all kinds of sports, reading books, and writing poems.
