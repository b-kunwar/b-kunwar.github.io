I am a graduate student in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at Auburn University and a research assistant in the Vehicle Systems, Dynamics, and Design Laboratory. I joined Auburn in Fall 2022.

I am fascinated by the possibility of aircraft learning autonomously from flight and improving their performance over time. My PhD research lays the groundwork by using flight data to understand aircraft behavior and refine simulation models and control systems.

After graduation, I hope to extend this work to enable continuous autonomous improvement from one flight to the next and explore how aircraft communicate their performance limitations (using AI) to both expert and non-expert users.

From 2019 to 2022, I worked as a drone design engineer at Prokura Innovations. With a small team of drone enthusiasts in Nepal, I designed, built, and tested a lift-plus-cruise vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) fixed-wing UAV to deliver emergency medicines to rural communities. This hands-on experience motivated me to pursue graduate study.

I was born and raised in a village in Baglung, Nepal. My education took me progressively farther from home, a journey that reminds me of a line from The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: “You were born to the rolling hills and little rivers of the Shire, but home is now behind you. The world is ahead.”

Outside of research, I love running, cycling, hiking, playing all kinds of sports, reading books, and writing poems.
